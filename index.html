Network On is a complete study app for two networking certifications: Cisco CCNA 200-301 and CompTIA Network+ N10-009. Lessons for every exam objective, quizzes with spaced repetition, a Cisco IOS style command-line simulator with labs, a subnetting trainer and timed exam simulations. Everything runs on your device: no account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking.

What is inside

  • Two curricula: CCNA (6 areas, 53 topics, 319 questions, 22 labs) and Network+ (5 areas, 25 objectives, 150 questions, 8 labs). Progress is kept per curriculum.
  • Lessons written for the exam, with configuration examples and an exam trap in every section. Explanations in English, German and Persian.
  • Quizzes with an explanation for every answer; wrong questions come back until they stick.
  • Command-line simulator: modes, abbreviations, ? help, tab completion and the real IOS error messages. show running-config reflects what you configured.
  • Labs in three levels (guided, free, troubleshooting). The app checks the device state, not your keystrokes.
  • Subnetting trainer with unlimited exercises and three difficulty levels.
  • Exam simulation with the official weighting, the real time limit and the real score scale.
